# VoltGuard-AI : Pinpointing Hidden Electricity Theft

Team F-35 · Hackspire Lumina 2026 · Domain 1: Predictive Infrastructure & Energy Analytics

VoltGuard AI helps electricity utilities find which premises in a leaking area is stealing electricity even when the thief’s own meter looks normal so inspectors visit a handful of premises instead of a whole neighbourhood.

The problem :
TNB lost RM4.57 billion to electricity theft at 13,827 premises between 2020 and August 2025, mostly linked to illegal cryptocurrency mining (The Vibes, 19 Nov 2025).
Syndicates tap cables before the meter, so the premises’ own reading looks normal.
Transformer meters show which area is leaking, but not which house.
Illegal wiring also exposes neighbours to fire and outage risks.
